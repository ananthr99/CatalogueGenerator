Typical Applications / Use Cases

Branch-Level Energy MonitoringProvides visibility into energy consumption across individual branches, helping organizations track usage patterns and compare performance between locations. This makes it easier to identify high-consumption sites, control energy costs, and improve operational efficiency.
Data Centre & Facility Load ProfilingAnalyzes power consumption patterns across data centres and facility infrastructure, including IT equipment, cooling systems, and critical loads. It helps identify peak demand periods, optimize load distribution, and improve energy efficiency and power availability.
Energy Cost AllocationTracks energy consumption across departments, equipment, or individual facilities to determine how electricity costs are distributed. This provides accurate cost visibility, simplifies internal billing, and helps organizations identify areas where energy expenses can be reduced.
